Research

My research interests sit at the intersection of signal processing, embedded sensing, machine learning, and computational approaches to understanding information in complex systems.

Maximizing von Neumann Entropy for Submodular Semi-Supervised Data Augmentation

Summer 2026
University of Delaware · Advisor: Dr. Austin Brockmeier
  • Undergraduate research in the Computational Neural Information Engineering Laboratory (CNIEL).
  • Encodes images with DINO feature representations and selects additional unlabeled examples per class to augment a labeled training set.
  • Optimizes the von Neumann entropy of the labeled set unioned with a selected unlabeled subset, using a scaled log-probability soft penalty to preserve likelihood constraints.
  • Research direction supports my broader interest in signal processing, machine learning, information-theoretic objectives, and computational methods for real-world sensing problems.
